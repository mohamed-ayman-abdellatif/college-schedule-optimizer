Quick Guide: How to Use the Optimizer

1
🏛️ Copy Schedule HTML from College Portal
  • Log in to your university portal (e.g. AASTMT Student Portal ➔ Schedule).
  • Select your term and choose "Course Schedule (جدول مقرر)" to view all class groups.
  • Select your Department and Subject (e.g. Applied Programming - ECE2102).
  • Right-click on the schedule table and select Inspect (or press F12 / Ctrl+Shift+I).
  • In the Elements DOM tree, right-click the <table> or the root <html> tag ➔ choose Copy ➔ Copy element.
💡 Pro Tip: You can copy the entire page HTML code (Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C in DevTools) and our smart parser will detect the schedule automatically!
2
📥 Paste & Add Courses into the Optimizer
  • Return to Tab "1. Add Courses" here in the optimizer.
  • Paste the copied HTML into the text box using Ctrl + V.
  • Click the purple "Parse & Add Course" button.
  • The app extracts all groups (B to L), lecture doctors with Dr. title, labs, and time periods!
  • Repeat for the other courses you want to register this semester.
3
👨‍🏫 Set Busy Hours & Doctor Preferences
  • Switch to Tab "2. Doctor & Goals".
  • Block Busy Times: Click or drag across the 16-period weekly grid to prohibit slots (e.g. sports training on Saturday 8:30 AM or commutes). Zero classes will ever be placed there!
  • Rank Doctors: Lecture professors are highlighted in alphabetical order at the top with a 🎓 Doctor badge. Click ⭐ Favorite for preferred professors or 🚫 Avoid to skip unwanted instructors.
  • Days Off: Select a target free day (e.g. Tuesday off) and set your gap minimization priority slider.
4
⚡ Generate & Export Your Ideal Timetable
  • Click the primary button "⚡ Find Best Schedules".
  • Browse clash-free options in the top carousel (e.g. Option #5: ⚡ 0 Gaps • 4 Days).
  • Inspect your weekly calendar with doctor names, period times, and coffee-break gap indicators (☕).
  • Export as PNG image for your phone, Google Calendar (.ics), or Print for registration!
