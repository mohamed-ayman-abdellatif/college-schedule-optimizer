Quick Guide: How to Use the Optimizer

1
Select Your Faculty or Import Schedule HTML / PDF
  • Choose your faculty, department, and period from the Major / Department Timetable tab and click Submit Subjects into Website.
  • Or copy the schedule table HTML from your student portal and paste it under Paste HTML Code, or upload a schedule PDF/screenshot.
2
Set Free Days, Blocked Times & Doctor Preferences
  • Open 2. Doctor & Goals to pick target days off and click or drag across the 16-period grid to block busy hours.
  • Mark professors as Favorite, triple-click to Mandate, or click Avoid to exclude them.
3
Generate, Filter & Export Your Schedule
  • Click Find Best Schedules to compute and rank all clash-free combinations.
  • Filter by maximum days, gaps, or classes per day, and export to PNG, TXT, .ics, or Print.
